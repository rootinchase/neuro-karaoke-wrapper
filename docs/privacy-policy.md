# Privacy Policy — Neuro Karaoke

**Effective date:** 28 July 2026
**App:** Neuro Karaoke (`com.neurokaraoke`)
**Developer:** aferil
**Contact:** aferil.official@gmail.com

Neuro Karaoke is an unofficial companion app for the website **neurokaraoke.com**. This policy explains what information the app handles and how. Please read it alongside the privacy practices of neurokaraoke.com, which is operated by the website's creator and is a separate service.

## Summary

- The app does **not** contain ads, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.
- The app does **not** sell or share your data for advertising.
- The only account data is what you provide by **signing in with Discord** (optional).
- Most of your settings and library data are stored **only on your device**.

## Information the app handles

### 1. Discord sign-in (optional)
If you choose to sign in with Discord, the app receives basic Discord account information — your Discord **user ID, username, and avatar** — through Discord's OAuth process. This is used to authenticate you and to link your favorites and playlists to your account. An authentication token is stored on your device to keep you signed in. You can use most of the app **without signing in**.

### 2. Favorites and playlists
When you are signed in, the songs you favorite and the playlists you create are sent to and stored on the **neurokaraoke.com** servers so they sync across your devices. You can remove favorites and playlist items in the app at any time.

### 3. Content requested while you use the app
To play music and show content, the app requests data from third-party services, which necessarily receive your device's IP address and request information:
- **neurokaraoke.com servers** (`api`, `idk`, `storage`, `images`, `socket`.neurokaraoke.com) — playlists, songs, audio, cover art, and account features.
- **Discord** (`discord.com`, `cdn.discordapp.com`) — sign-in and your avatar image.
- **Last.fm** (`lastfm.freetls.fastly.net`) — artist images.
- **LRCLIB** (`lrclib.net`) — song lyrics, when not available from neurokaraoke.com.
- **YouTube image CDN** (`i.ytimg.com`) — cover art thumbnails.
- **Radio stream host** (`radio.twinskaraoke.com`) — live radio audio.

### 4. Information stored only on your device
The following are kept locally on your device and are **not** collected by the developer:
- App settings (theme, equalizer, playback preferences)
- Recently played and playback position
- Downloaded songs and cached audio, images, and lyrics (for offline and smooth playback)

## Permissions the app uses

- **Internet / network** — to stream and download content.
- **Foreground service (media playback)** and **notifications** — for background playback and lock-screen/media controls.
- **Car app media templates** — for Android Auto and Android Automotive.

The app does **not** request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos.

## Data retention and deletion

- **On-device data** (settings, downloads, cache) is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
- **Account data** (favorites, playlists) stored on neurokaraoke.com can be removed in the app, or by contacting the developer at the address above to request deletion of data associated with your account.

## Children

Neuro Karaoke is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
